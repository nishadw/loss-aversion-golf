# loss-aversion-golf

**How loss-averse are golfers, and what predicts it?**

Loss aversion is a conservative bias that costs strokes: a player who fears a bogey more
than they want a birdie aims away from pins they should attack. This surveys a pool of
golfers, measures the degree of loss aversion in each, and fits it against participant
attributes to find what predicts it.

The practical output is the design of campaigns to help a player or a team correct for the
bias once it has been measured.

Research conducted with Professor Krista Hill-Cummings.
